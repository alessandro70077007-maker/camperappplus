CAMPERappPLUS

The desktop app to manage your camper: deadlines, digital logbook, trips, fuel, parking sites and weather. All in one place, free.

⬇️ Download for Windows (106 MB) v1.4 · Windows 10/11 · Works offline (except map and weather)

Everything a camper owner needs

One app to never forget anything — from deadlines to parking sites, from fuel stats to weather.

⏰

Deadlines under control

MOT, insurance, road tax, gas bottles, service: automatic email or desktop notifications when a deadline is coming.

🔧

Digital logbook

Maintenance history with category, cost and km. Export to PDF for the next owner or workshop.

📍

Parking sites on the map

Find caravan sites, sanitary dump stations, campsites and green areas nearby. Free data from OpenStreetMap, interactive map.

🌤️

Built-in weather

Current conditions and 5-day forecast at your location or any city you search by name.

⛽

Real fuel stats

Log refills, get accurate l/100km between full fillups. Per-camper and over-time statistics.

🗺️

Trip diary

Record every trip with destination, km, cost. Stats on totals and cost per km.

📎

Documents at hand

Upload registration, insurance, receipts, photos. All linked to the right camper — no more messy folders.

✅

Custom checklists

Lists for departure, setup, teardown, maintenance. Resettable at the end of each trip.

🌍

5 languages

Italian, English, German, French, Spanish. Switch on the fly from settings.

📱

Phone companion

Open the app on your phone via QR (same WiFi). Installable as a PWA, with auto-refresh of data shared with the PC.

Your data stays yours

No account, no cloud, no tracking. CAMPERappPLUS runs on your PC.

🔒

100% local

All data and documents live on your PC, in %APPDATA%\CamperAppPlus.

📦

Full backup

Export everything to a ZIP (DB + attachments) to switch PC without losing anything.

🎁

Free, no account

No signup, no subscription, no ads.

📡

Only public data online

Internet used only for weather (Open-Meteo) and map (OpenStreetMap). Your data goes nowhere.

Install in 3 steps

No complicated setup: download, extract, launch.

📋 Requirements

  • Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)
  • Microsoft Edge (included with Windows)
  • ~250 MB disk space
  • Internet connection (only for map and weather)

🚀 Launch

  1. Download the file CamperAppPlus_v1.4_windows.zip
  2. Right-click the zip → Extract All
  3. Open the extracted folder and double-click CamperAppPlus.exe
⚠️ SmartScreen warning: the first time Windows may show "Windows protected your PC". This is normal for unsigned apps. Click More info → Run anyway.
